A fix for the desktop app's Comfy key, Nano Banana 2.1 on Gemini and on Comfy Router, and the Router's catalog brought up to date.

**Download:** [Mac · Apple Silicon](https://nodebanana.app/download/mac) (macOS 13 or later) or [Windows · x64](https://nodebanana.app/download/windows) (Windows 10 or later). Installed Mac apps update themselves from this release. The Windows installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen warns on the first run; say **More info → Run anyway**. Bring your own provider keys: Node Banana calls the providers directly and their usage is billed to you by them.

## Fixed

**"Keys could not be saved securely: Invalid credential field."** On the desktop, entering the Comfy key under Settings → Providers refused the whole save, and with it any other key saved at the same time, because the secure store did not know that provider's name. It does now. If you hit this in 2.1.0, enter the key again after updating.

## Added

- **Nano Banana 2.1 on Gemini.** Google's Nano Banana 2.1 joins the Generate Image node's Gemini models, at 1K, 2K or 4K, with Nano Banana 2's wide 1:4 to 8:1 ratios and its Google and Image Search grounding, from $0.034 an image.
- **Comfy Router models brought up to date.** Nano Banana 2.1; Pika's own routes for Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2, GPT Image 2, Pika 2.5 (text and image to video) and Seedance 2.0; and Higgsfield's Genjutsu Motion Transfer, which recasts a video with up to eight reference images. Imagen 3, Imagen 3 Fast, Veo 3 and Veo 3 Fast, which the Router no longer serves, are gone.

## Verify a download

Each release lists `SHA256SUMS-mac-arm64.txt` and `SHA256SUMS-windows-x64.txt`; compare with `shasum -a 256 <file>` on the Mac or `Get-FileHash <file>` in PowerShell.
